Grammar Detective Short: MATERIAL
Narration: George (British English, male)

[0:00.35] Is a house made of brick, or bricks? Both are correct.

[0:04.88] Think of gold. A ring, a coin and a necklace are three things. Gold is the substance they are made from. So we ask: how much gold?

[0:14.55] I count three rings, or measure twenty grams of gold. The number goes on rings or grams, not on gold.

[0:23.12] Now the house. ‘Made of bricks’ means individual blocks you could count. ‘Made of brick’ names the material. Both are right. The difference is what the word names in your sentence.
