Narration: George (British English, male)

[0:00.35] Why is ‘The furniture are expensive’ wrong?

[0:04.42] Furniture contains many things: a chair, a table, a sofa, a bed.

[0:10.15] But English groups them together, and names them as one unit.

[0:15.08] Unit means one. And one is always singular. We can’t add s to one.

[0:20.52] So: ‘The furniture is expensive.’
