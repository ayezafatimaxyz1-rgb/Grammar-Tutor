Grammar Detective Short: ACTIVITY / PROCESS
Narration: Emma (British English, female)

[0:00.35] Why ‘a lot of work’, but ‘four tasks’?

[0:03.48] She types, plans, calls and cleans. Several actions. But work, meaning her daily effort, names all of that as one ongoing activity.

[0:13.12] So: ‘She does a lot of work every day.’ Not ‘many works’.

[0:17.68] To count the actions, choose another noun: ‘She completes four tasks every day.’

[0:23.48] And ‘three works of art’? Correct. Here works means created pieces. The meaning has changed, so the grammar changes too.
