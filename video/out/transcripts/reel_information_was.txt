[0:00.35] Spot the error. A classic CSS and PMS pattern.

[0:08.38] There are actually two mistakes here, and one logic fixes both.

[0:13.55] Information is abstract. Facts, news, details: you can’t hold information in your hand, and you can’t count it. So it’s uncountable: no s. Information, never informations.

[0:26.58] And because it’s uncountable, it works as one whole, so the verb is singular too: was, not were.

[0:34.02] ‘The information provided by the officer was not accurate.’ Need a number? Say a piece of information.
