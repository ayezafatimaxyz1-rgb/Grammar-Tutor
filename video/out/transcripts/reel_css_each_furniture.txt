Narration: George (British English, male)

[0:00.35] This sentence came in a CSS exam. Can you find the error?

[0:08.65] Most students attack the verb ‘is’. But the verb is fine. The real problem is the word ‘each’.

[0:16.08] ‘Each’ means we pick things one by one: each chair, each table, each sofa. So ‘each’ needs something you can count.

[0:24.72] But furniture is not one object. It’s a unit: chairs, tables and sofas grouped together and named as one. A unit means one, and you can’t pick ‘each’ out of one.

[0:36.52] So we give ‘each’ something countable to hold: a piece. Each piece of furniture.

[0:42.65] ‘Each piece of furniture in this display is on sale for half price.’

[0:48.38] Logic, not rules. Comment ‘logic’ for the next one.
