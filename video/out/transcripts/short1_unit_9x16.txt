Grammar Detective Short: UNIT / MASS
Narration: Emma (British English, female)

[0:00.35] Four objects. So why does furniture take ‘is’?

[0:03.98] A chair, a table, a sofa and a bed. I can count them: one chair, two chairs. But furniture is the overall label for the whole collection, not the name of each item.

[0:15.25] English treats that word as uncountable. So: ‘The furniture is expensive.’ Not ‘are’.

[0:21.68] Need a number? Count pieces: ‘Two pieces of furniture are expensive.’ The number goes on pieces, so the verb is plural.

[0:30.38] Same picture with luggage: ‘The luggage is heavy’, but ‘three pieces of luggage are heavy’. See what the noun names, then choose the verb.
