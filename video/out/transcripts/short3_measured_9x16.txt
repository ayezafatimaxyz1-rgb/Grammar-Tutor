Grammar Detective Short: MEASURED
Narration: Emma (British English, female)

[0:00.35] There are thousands of grains. So why not ‘many rice’?

[0:04.58] Zoom in and you see grains. But when I say rice as food, I mean the amount in the bowl, not a list of grains.

[0:11.92] So: ‘I ate too much rice for lunch.’ Not ‘too many rice’.

[0:16.52] Want to count? Choose a unit: ‘many grains of rice’, ‘two bowls of rice’. The number goes onto grains or bowls.

[0:24.15] Bread works the same way: ‘three slices of bread’. Picture the amount, then pick the unit.
