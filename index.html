About Sarwar's English Lab

Sarwar's English Lab is founded and led by Mahmood Sarwar — a LUMS graduate and Certified English Language Teacher — for anyone learning or refining their English: students preparing for exams, professionals polishing their writing, and lifelong learners who just want grammar to finally make sense.

Every lesson is written to be understood in under a minute, with examples pulled from real writing and conversation rather than dusty textbook sentences.
